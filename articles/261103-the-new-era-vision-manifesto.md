# 261103 - The New ERA Vision - Manifesto

# The New ERA Vision


*A manifesto: Dr. Thomas* | 07 *November 2026*


---


## The Root of the Matter


America does not lack weapons. It lacks will. And it lacks will because it has forgotten what is worth sacrificing for.


For nearly eighty years we have been taught that faith is private, that righteousness is a matter of taste, and that the name of Christ does not belong in the public square. We learned the lesson well. We now have a nation rich in power and poor in endurance—able to strike any target on earth, and unable to sustain a costly course for longer than an election cycle.


Every great danger before us—a revolutionary regime reaching for the bomb, machines that may outgrow their makers, plagues engineered in laboratories, the slow retreat of the civilization that carries the Bible—can be slowed by laws and technical controls. None of them stays solved while the people who must uphold those laws believe in nothing beyond their own comfort.


The root of our trouble is spiritual. So the cure must be spiritual first. *“If my people who are called by my name humble themselves, and pray and seek my face and turn from their wicked ways, then I will hear from heaven and will forgive their sin and heal their land”* **(2 Chronicles 7:14)**.


The New ERA Vision is a call to that turning, and a plan for what a turned nation can do.


## What the Letters Mean


**E is for Energy.** Abundant, affordable, American energy—so that no tyrant can hold our economy hostage, and no family must choose between heat and food. Its foundation is nuclear power, built at full speed, and thorium molten-salt reactors brought from design to operation. Energy is the one victory over our enemies that requires no target list.


**R is for Righteousness.** A people pleasing to God. Not perfection, but a decisive turn: the flesh made to wait upon the Spirit, in the home, in trade, and in the public square. *“Righteousness exalts a nation, but sin is a reproach to any people”* **(Proverbs 14:34)**. Righteousness is what makes our witness worth hearing and our will strong enough to last.


**A is for Accountability.** Leaders who answer to God, to the law, and to the people. Wars declared by Congress, not drifted into. Costs told honestly before they are paid. Juries that remember they judge the law as well as the facts. Rulers who live under the rules they make.


## The Test of Every Policy


Every proposal in this Vision must pass three questions, in this order:


- **Is it righteous?** Does it accord with what God establishes and judges good?

- **If so, is it loving?** Does it seek the true good of my neighbor—not his momentary appetite, but his soul’s lasting welfare? Humoring a neighbor is not love: *“You shall reason frankly with your neighbor”* **(Leviticus 19:17)**.

- **Would I want it done to me?** Not as I am in a moment of desire, but as a man committed to righteousness, seeing his life from eternity.


A policy that fails the first question is not rescued by the second. A policy that passes all three is worth fighting for.


## The First Case: Iran


The war with Iran is where this Vision meets the present hour.


The men who rule Iran will not make peace in good faith. We cannot afford the war it would take to remove them by force. So we must hold the line while hearts change—theirs and ours.


**What we can do today:** Keep the blockade. Defend our ships. Cut off the regime’s money, not the people’s bread. Strike its nuclear and missile sites when we find them, and again when they rebuild. Spare the water and power that keep children alive. Build safe ways for men inside the program to tell us what they know. Offer one condition, and only one: *Let us in, let us dig out the uranium, and the bombs stop falling on your heads.* Ask Congress to vote clear red lines. Take away the oil weapon, honestly telling the people what it will cost.


**What it will take to end it:** The Gospel of Christ carried to the Persian people—spoken openly by our President, carried on roads our nation builds, and delivered in their own tongue by Persian believers. And a change in us: a nation that has stopped being afraid to name the source of its freedom, and has started living the faith it would offer to others.


The full plan is set out in *Winning the Long War with Iran*.


## The Other Dangers on the Horizon


Iran is the nearest danger, not the only one. The same Vision applies to each of the others.


**Artificial intelligence.** A power that may outstrip its makers must be governed by men who know they are not God. Technical restraints are necessary. They will hold only as long as the people who write them believe that human beings bear the image of their Creator and must not be made servants of their own machines.


**Engineered plagues.** Watchfulness, attribution, and hard limits on dangerous research can slow this threat. Only a people who fear God more than they love discovery will keep those limits when the next breakthrough tempts them.


**The retreat of our civilization.** A culture that will not marry, will not bear children, and will not pass on its faith is choosing to end. No immigration law or tax credit can fix what is at root a loss of hope. Hope returns when a people believes again that its life has a purpose beyond itself.


We should not wait until any of these dangers is at the door. By then, wise action may no longer be enough, and only a miracle will do. The time to act is while the threat is on the horizon.


## The Freedom to Speak


The prison that holds us is not in the Constitution. It is in our minds.


The First Amendment forbids a national church and the coercion of conscience. It does not command public silence about God. The men who wrote it opened Congress with prayer. In Jefferson’s day the Senate ratified a treaty that paid toward a priest and a church. The Supreme Court has begun again to read the Establishment Clause by the history of the founders, not by the doctrine invented in 1947.


What remains is fear: the fear of being called a bigot, a zealot, a crusader. The New ERA Vision rejects that fear. We will speak the Gospel plainly, because liberty that cannot be spoken is not liberty, and a nation that will not name the source of its freedom cannot offer that freedom to anyone else.


## The Family, the School, and the Community


The turning begins in the home. Marriage kept, children raised in the knowledge of God, appetites ordered by His law. No government can do this for a family. A government can stop working against it.


Education belongs to the people closest to the child. School boards govern schools, not Washington. The President’s part is to lead by voice: to urge every community to teach its children truth, to remove boards that teach them confusion about who they are, and to refuse federal money for doing so.


## Who I Am, and Why I Run


I am one citizen. I take no donations. I run as a witness, to say in public what I believe a nation must hear, and to show what a platform built on righteousness would look like.


I hold every position in this Vision as an argument, not a decree. I will defend each one with reasons, and I will change my mind for a better argument. But I will not soften the claim at the center: Jesus Christ is Lord, and a nation that turns to Him will find the strength that God provides.


## The Call


This Vision does not begin in Washington. It begins in your house.


- **Turn.** Order your own life by the standard you would ask of the nation.

- **Speak.** Say in public what you believe in private, without apology and without fear.

- **Send.** Read the position papers. Send them to your representatives, your senators, the President, and the Secretary of War. Tell them to look in their inbox. Send them to your circle, and ask your circle to send them on.

- **Endure.** The regime in Tehran, and every enemy like it, is betting that we will tire. Become a person who does not tire, and help build a nation that does not.


If the followers lead, the leaders will follow.


Energy. Righteousness. Accountability.


**The New ERA Vision.**
