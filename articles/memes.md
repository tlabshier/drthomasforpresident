# Memes

Don’t participate in Evil,

Don’t fund the Enemy,

Don’t live in Fear

Radiate Love

Make Memes

TheMedicalRebel.com Lee Merrit, MD


**Commentary by Dr. Thomas:**

* Love your enemies by returning good for evil. Speak the truth in love.

* Don’t participate in evil by knowing the definition of evil by studying the Bible.

* Use fear to warn of danger and proceed as safely as possible. If evil is causing fear, then oppose evil strategically, gather coalitions, be a leader, speak the politically incorrect truth, and be a martyr if you are called. The reward of standing for Godliness on earth and sacrificing for Him is great.

* Don’t participate in evil activities that fund the enemy.

* Speak Biblical memes, to encourage, instruct, and remind others of good/right/Godly behavior.
