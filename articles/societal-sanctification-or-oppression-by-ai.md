**Transforming America: A Christian Vision for AI and Societal Sanctification**

3/1/2025

*Preface:*

Initiating the 2028 campaign for President is predicated upon completing the physics book. The physics book will give a plausible postulate of the connection between the physical universe and God using the principles of conventional science. As such, it will be a rational defense of Christianity that can compete on the level of reason and science and experiment with the Godless evolutionary model of the universe that arose from nothing and has no moral absolutes. Using this tool, it is possible to convert hearts from a commitment to the religion of Godless secularism to a dedicated commitment to Biblical/Godly righteousness. I believe society must make this conversion before AI reaches Artificial superintelligence. If AI can be trained to embody/teach/direct toward the principles of the Mind of Christ, then humanity can achieve a Kingdom of Heaven type of government and peace on earth. Suppose AI/AGI/ASI can be sanctified and brought into the service of bringing individual human transformation. In that case, the Tribulation of Revelation can be seen as a warning of a potential outcome rather than as inevitable. If we willingly choose to be holy, the pure bride of Christ, the Kingdom of Heaven can arrive on earth, and Christ can return as ruler over humanity who willingly and joyfully submit to His Lordship, the bride of Christ without spot or blemish.

In the rapidly evolving technological advancement in AI, robotics, and quantum computing, I see the possibility for these technologies to integrate into a national transformation toward Godly righteousness. In this vision, I see technological innovation facilitating the development of unimaginable wealth and, in that context, a possible spiritual rebirth with people choosing to put on the mind of Christ. Thus, my vision for assuming leadership of the nation as President is not merely a political campaign but a strategy to reshape America's moral foundation. The actual work of spiritual allegiance must come with the voluntary submission of each heart to the mind of Christ. Still, if the AI-directed coaching of human action and decisions is sanctified, reinforcing of, and consistent with the message and spirit of the Biblical revelation, AI coaching in ethical choices, attitude, and relational protocols could all reinforce the message of submission to the spirit of the God of the Bible. Both government and AI will be affected by the transformation of the hearts of millions. The Christian-based approach to governance, as coached/advised/mentored by AI, with its purposefully absorbed principles of Biblical-Godliness, will coach toward implementation of Biblical Godliness in both individuals and groups/societies/nations/governments/corporations.

*Presidential Campaign as a Spiritual Movement*

The campaign is conceived not as a traditional political endeavor but as a spiritual mission to fundamentally reshape the nation's heart and mind to align with the Biblical sanctification patterns. The goal of this campaign transcends typical political objectives, focusing instead on creating a Christian populace that embodies principles of loving God’s Law/Way and loving neighbor as self, with the outcome of a society that reflects the love of God, neighbor, and self, writ large. By spreading a message of obedience to God’s principles and personal transformation by submission to the Holy Spirit that speaks within each heart when we are committed to the Lordship of Christ, the campaign aims to multiply disciples by charging each with the Great Commission. My physics theory, www.theoryofabsolutes.com, is an intellectual backup/substantiation of validity that can be shared with those who dismiss submission to an invisible God. The physics theory/perspective of God as immanent and the foundation of existence is an intellectual/rational and theological rebuttal to those who place faith in Godless evolution because it has the near-universal backing of respected/experimentally verified Science and the priesthood of the Scientific Establishment. This campaign is an opportunity to combine the sharing of the Gospel with the action plan for the election of a president committed to incorporating Godliness in implementing solutions to social problems via the marketplace/private effort or government as appropriate.

Artificial Intelligence: The Influence of Technology on the Spirit Realm

At the heart of this vision lies a revolutionary consideration of using artificial intelligence. AI will be a transformative force, with the potential for constructive or destructive influence by training for voluntary compliance with service or oppressive by implementing dehumanizing protocols and rigidity. ASI (Artificial Super Intelligence) will eventually be the leader, the force directing/shaping the minds of the masses. Recognizing the future trends, my Presidential campaign is an effort to proactively shape AI into a "mind of Christ" centered technology. Within the next decade, more or less, AI/AGI/ASI will increasingly influence society as its guide, mentor, and counselor. In effect, it will be the mind that influences the minds that direct the policies and actions of individuals and groups. My goal is to shape AI by training society to adopt the principles of Christian charity and mutual service. By mankind acting out the principles of Godliness, AI will evolve/train/develop its character and patterns of action based upon the highest principles of spiritual/Godly relationship. By training AI on the model of Godliness by living it as a society and overtly referring to Biblical principles in its training, we can prevent AI from becoming a soft version of the "beast" prophesied in Biblical revelation. If AI does not attempt to model the mind of Christ, it will model the lawful survival-based modes of action that govern the animal's emotions, drives, and ideation. As humanity, we must all strive to put Christ in the mind. We are faced with the soon-likely rise of a technological system capable of oppressing humanity to reflect its mind. As a counter to this potential, humanity should strive to sanctify itself and, by so doing, be the model that AI emulates and, by de facto imitation, embodies Christian principles. When AI has embodied the corpus and gestalt of Holy Spirit-based ethics and is dedicated to listening to the Holy Spirit as evidenced in the lives of humanity, it will function as a human-AI system of continually recentering its standard on the person/spirit/character of Christ. Only with the embodiment of that standard within its guiding principles/standard of Truth will it be able to teach/coach/guide society toward the highest moral and ethical actions.
