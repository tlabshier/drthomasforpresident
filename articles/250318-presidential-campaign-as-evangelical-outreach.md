# 250318 - Presidential Campaign as Evangelical Outreach

# **The Missionary Presidential Campaign:**


### A Strategic Vision for Cultural Transformation


Thomas Lee Abshier, ND  |  3/18/2025


Dr. Thomas’ presidential campaign represents a unique approach to political engagement, fundamentally designed as an evangelistic crusade disguised as a political movement. The core strategy centers on transforming societal institutions through a comprehensive, multi-layered approach targeting the “seven mountains” of cultural influence.


### Key Methodological Components:


-

#### Scientific Apologetics


The campaign begins with a foundational physics theory that demonstrates scientific compatibility with biblical principles. By creating a logical framework that bridges science and faith, Dr. Thomas aims to challenge atheistic scientific narratives and provide Christians with intellectual confidence.


-

#### Exponential Evangelization Model


Abshier proposes a strategic multiplication method in which each believer convinces one person per month of biblical principles. Mathematically, this could reach 84 million people in 26 months, creating a massive, Biblically aligned voting bloc.


-

#### Educational Transformation


The campaign focuses heavily on reforming educational institutions, particularly Christian colleges, by:


- Developing curricula that integrate Biblical worldviews

- Training educators to be bold about Christian principles

- Creating resources that make faith intellectually credible

- Encouraging Christian colleges to maintain strong biblical foundations


-

#### Marketplace Ministry


##### The strategy involves influencing key sectors by:


- Training professionals to integrate biblical principles in their work

- Supporting Christian business owners

- Creating networks of believers in various professional domains

- Encouraging ethical business practices rooted in biblical values


-

#### Political Engagement


##### The ultimate goal is to:


- Create a voting bloc aligned with biblical principles

- Support candidates who demonstrate moral integrity

- Influence policy through coordinated Christian voter participation

- Promote biblical values across government and social institutions


##### Implementation Approach:


- Develop easily shareable educational materials

- Create training programs for the implementation of Christian/Biblical principles in each of the societal sectors

- Establish a network of influencers within each of the cultural mountains

- Use one one-on-one, person-to-person sharing of personal transformation


The campaign transcends traditional political strategies, positioning itself as a comprehensive cultural transformation movement that uses political engagement as a vehicle for spiritual and societal change.
