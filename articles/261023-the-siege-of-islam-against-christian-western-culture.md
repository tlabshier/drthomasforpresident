# 261023 - The Siege of Islam Against Christian Western Culture

# The Siege and the Witness


*Dr. Thomas Abshier — October 2026*


*From a conversation with my friend Andree, who has traveled to Israel some thirty times and whose friends there are Jews, Messianic Jews, and Christians who have lived their whole lives in the land.*


---


## The stand I take


I stand on this proposition: the Western nations are under a patient, organized siege by a political movement that wants to replace their law with its own, and the decisive weapon in that siege is not the bomb but the false witness. The movement is Islamism, the political program that seeks to bring nations under Islamic law, and in the streets it has found a working ally in the revolutionary left, whose goals are different but whose enemy is the same: the Judeo-Christian God and order of law, conscience, and free speech that made America.


I want to be precise about who the enemy is. It is not the Muslim family down the street, who came here to work, raise children, and live in peace. Many of them fled exactly the regimes I describe. The enemy is a program, and you can name it, study it, and resist it by lawful means without hating a single neighbor.


## What Andree has watched


Andree has been watching this for thirty years. In the 1990s she met Avi Lipkin, who writes as Victor Mordecai, at the Christian Embassy in Jerusalem, and stayed in his home. His wife was born a Jew in Egypt; her family was driven out after Israel’s rebirth in 1948, along with roughly 850,000 Jews expelled or pressured out of Arab lands from Morocco to Iraq to Yemen. No one calls their loss a catastrophe or keeps them in refugee camps for seventy-five years. For years, she monitored Arab broadcasts for Israeli radio, and what she heard went into her husband’s books and his talks in American churches. His message was simple: what is said in Arabic about the West is not what is said in English, and it is coming here.


What struck Andree most was how quickly the reversal happened after October 7, 2023. On the day of the massacre, the world mourned with Israel. Within a day, rallies filled the streets of American cities, campus groups issued statements, and signs were ready. Before Israel’s ground forces had entered Gaza, Israel had become, in the eyes of the young, the genocidal aggressor. The communities that Hamas destroyed along the Gaza border were among the most pro-Palestinian in Israel: people who drove sick Gazans to hospitals and hired them on their farms. They were killed anyway. That fact taught the Israeli left something the Western left has not yet learned.


Her friends in Israel, now mostly in their seventies, were amazed by something else: the generation of eighteen-to-twenty-year-olds who, when the call came, stood and fought for their country and gave their lives for it, many of them, in a war fought largely underground, through tunnel networks that Israeli estimates put at several hundred miles, built in large part with diverted Western aid.


## The patient method


Andree described a sequence of infiltrating and gaining influence over Western culture and institutions that she has heard from people who have studied it: The Muslim will first run for and become an official in the school board, then the county council, then the legislature, then the courts, and only when the community is strong enough, open confrontation. This is not a conspiracy theory invented by its critics. In 1991, a member of the Muslim Brotherhood in America wrote an internal memorandum describing the Brotherhood’s work in North America as a “civilization-jihadist process,” a long project of undermining Western civilization from within by its own institutions. That document was entered into evidence in the Holy Land Foundation terrorism-financing trial in 2008, which ended in convictions. The constitution of the Islamic Republic of Iran, in its own preamble, charges its armed forces with extending the sovereignty of God’s law throughout the world. These are the movement’s own words.


Now, there is nothing wrong, in principle, with patience or with running for school board. Christians may do exactly the same thing, and should. Every movement that persuades a people does it one office at a time. The difference is not in the patience. The difference is in the means. A movement willing to burn, to lie about why it burns, and to treat outsiders’ deception as permitted in service of its goal holds an advantage over one that is not, until the people learn to see the method.


## The riot as advertisement


Why does the violence work? It should discredit the cause. Instead, it advertises it.


The news business has always known that if it bleeds, it leads. The animal part within us, can’t look away from the fire, the wreck on the shoulder that backs up traffic for miles, even though nothing blocks the road. We watch murder for entertainment. The Muslim arsonist understands this. He sets the fire, and while the cameras come to watch, he holds up his sign: *Genocide. From the river to the sea.* And the message he sends with the fire is this: *Yes, what we did was terrible, but look how evil you must be to have driven us to it.* Seeing the passion, the viewer concludes the grievance must be real. The criminal becomes the hero, and his victim becomes the accused.


We saw this last weekend in France. What began weeks ago as high-school protests over overcrowded, underfunded schools grew on October 17 into nationwide cost-of-living demonstrations of half a million or more, and by nightfall into the worst urban violence France has seen in decades: a town hall in Limoges nearly destroyed by fire, the tourist office in Lyon burned, a prefect’s residence in Bordeaux attacked, some 2,000 shops looted or damaged, a synagogue in Paris broken into, Jewish-owned shops burned in Marseille, more than 2,000 arrests. Through it all, in Paris and Milan, Palestinian flags flew. Students’ grievances about their schools were real. But the flags tell you who had arrived to use them.


Andree told me of a young man in her extended family who lived in Portland during the 2020 riots. He was a committed left-wing man, a Bernie Sanders voter. He went out to photograph the riots, and he met young men who told him they had been brought in from Chicago and paid. Then he watched a group dressed in black beat a Christian young man who had knelt to pray over someone who was hurt. He left the left that night. Nothing he had read changed his mind. What changed it was seeing with his own eyes.


## The counterfeit of two or three witnesses


That is the key. Scripture sets the standard of evidence: at the mouth of two or three witnesses shall every matter be established (Deuteronomy 19:15). It is a good standard. It is how a people knows what is true without seeing everything for itself.


But the standard can be counterfeited. If the same narrative is repeated by activists in the street, by the newspaper, by the network, by the professor, and by the politician, it meets the standard of two or three witnesses, even though none of them has seen anything. It becomes a wall of agreement, a consensus, a cultural meme. We now “simply know” what is true. And the young man who actually saw the beating in Portland is one witness against a thousand echoes.


## The mind in the sky


Now we have added something more powerful than the newspaper. In my own words, spoken in the conversation:

>
We now have AI, which reads all of the news, and the news has been established in this way. So when someone comes along and says, “I saw this happening,” the machine answers: that’s false, that’s been debunked; here are the statistics, here is what the research centers say, here is what the news media say, here is what the court records show, and all of it points to the conclusion that this is misinformation, a right-wing conspiracy theory. So now the expert, the mind in the sky that knows everything, amplifies and validates the manufactured consensus and quashes every pocket of resistance. The first-person account, the man who went out and saw it, is not reported, not amplified, not put into the databases. The outliers become examples of misinformation. Then we pass hate-speech laws and misinformation rules, and the person on social media who says “I saw this” becomes an enemy of the truth, vilified and marginalized as an example of the kind of people who are now the enemy.

I have written on this site about the danger AI poses to human freedom. This is one of its quietest forms. A machine trained on consensus will defend it, and it will do so in the calm, footnoted voice of authority. The remedy is not a machine that says the opposite. The remedy is a machine, and a press, and a government, that present contested questions as contested, show the evidence on each side, and preserve the first-hand witness instead of erasing him.


I will also say plainly: an honest machine can help. While preparing this article, we checked the evidence, and several things Andree and I said in conversation turned out to be overstated or wrong. The notes below correct them. A witness who corrects his own errors is a stronger witness, not a weaker one.


## Why the concern remains plausible


Some will say all of this is fear of the stranger dressed up as analysis. I do not believe it is, for four reasons.


**The movement says what it intends.** The Brotherhood memorandum, the Iranian constitution, and the chants of “globalize the intifada” on American campuses are not interpretations imposed by critics. They are statements the movement itself makes.


**The doctrine and behavior align.** In 2015, the Islamic State filmed the beheading of twenty-one Christian workers on a Libyan beach; one of them, not originally a Christian, reportedly chose to die with the others, saying their God was his God. The Revelation speaks of the souls of those beheaded for the witness of Jesus (Revelation 20:4). Beheading as a religious punishment is practiced today by one movement. And the newest lie, now circulating among some fringe Christian teachers online, that it will be the Jews who behead Christians, is exactly the kind of inversion this siege depends on, and exactly the kind of claim that, repeated often enough, an AI will one day report as “a view held by some.”


**The money moves in the same direction.** Foreign governments, including Gulf states that promote Islamist causes, have given American universities billions of dollars, and the universities are where the signs were printed. Western aid sent to Gaza for schools and water became tunnels. Paid protesters are not a myth; companies openly sell crowds.


**The pattern repeats.** Europe went down this road first. France has lived with Islamist terrorism for decades. Britain’s institutions now hesitate to say plainly what they see, for fear of riots. America, as Andree put it, is Britain’s younger sister, a few years behind on the same path.


## Where the record is uncertain


I hold my position, and these are the places where the information is incomplete or where our conversation went beyond the evidence.


The San Diego action Andree heard about is real, but it is not criminal law. On September 15, 2026, the San Diego City Council unanimously adopted a nonbinding resolution defining “Islamophobia” as an educational tool, months after two young white nationalists murdered three men at the Islamic Center of San Diego. Criticizing Islam is not a crime in San Diego. My concern remains: the Council on American-Islamic Relations drafted the definition, and it will be distributed to every city department, including the police. A government definition of forbidden opinion, handed to the police, is how speech codes begin, whatever the intention.


The French riots were not, at their root, an Islamic uprising. They grew from student and cost-of-living protests, and French authorities have said some of the damage to Jewish property was theft rather than hatred. But the Palestinian flags were everywhere, and a movement does not need to start a fire to warm its hands at it.

## What I would do


My test for any policy has three questions. Is it righteous? If so, is it loving? Would I want it done to me? Applied here:


**One standard for speech.** Government should punish acts — violence, threats, vandalism, discrimination in employment and housing — and never opinions about any religion. That includes criticism of Islam, of Judaism, and of Christianity. I would oppose any federal, state, or municipal speech code, and I would make clear that federal funds may not support enforcing one. Every mosque, synagogue, and church deserves the same protection from violence. A Muslim neighbor should feel loved by that law; so should the man who wants to criticize the Quran and Muhammad. I would want both protections for myself.


**A riot is not speech.** The federal Anti-Riot Act already makes it a crime to travel or organize across state lines to incite a riot. I would enforce it against organizers and funders, not only against the teenager with the brick, and equally against left and right.


**Admit neighbors.** Our immigration law already bars those who advocate the overthrow of the government or belong to totalitarian movements. I would enforce it seriously, with vetting that asks whether the newcomer intends to live under the Constitution or to replace it. That is not hostility to Muslims. Every nation has the right to ask every guest this question.


**Expose the money.** Federal law already requires universities to disclose large foreign gifts. I would enforce it, publish the totals, and let parents decide where to send their children.


**Protect the witness from the machine.** I would oppose any government program that directs AI companies or social-media platforms to suppress “misinformation” on contested political questions, and I would require AI systems used by the federal government to present contested claims as contested. The first-hand witness must not be erased by the consensus.


**Bear true witness.** The final remedy is not law. A false witness is answered by a true one. The young man in Portland changed because he saw. Christians, Jews, and honest people of every faith must go and see, and then say what they saw, calmly and without hatred, again and again.


The siege is patient. The defenders must be even more patient. When Nehemiah rebuilt the walls of Jerusalem under threat, each builder worked with one hand and held a weapon in the other (Nehemiah 4:17). Our weapon is the truth, told plainly, and the law, applied equally. I offer this position open to a better argument, but I hold it until one comes.

## Notes on the record


These are corrections and qualifications to claims made in the conversation, added after checking.


- **Campus encampments.** Rallies and campus statements began within a day of October 7, 2023, but large-tent encampments at Columbia and other universities began in April 2024. Israel’s ground offensive in Gaza began in late October 2023 (about October 27).

- **British mayors.** London has had a Muslim mayor, Sadiq Khan, since 2016, and several British cities have had Muslim lord mayors (largely ceremonial, rotating posts). The claim that every major British city has a Muslim mayor is not accurate.

- **King Charles.** In 1994, Charles said he would prefer to be seen as “Defender of Faith” rather than “Defender of the Faith.” On his accession in 2022, he swore the traditional oaths to uphold the Church of England while pledging to protect all faiths.

- **France.** The riots of October 17–18, 2026 are documented by Reuters, AP, Euronews, JTA and others. A town hall in Limoges, nearly destroyed by fire, was reported; I could not confirm a historic tower burned in Toulouse, nor the report of a looted grocery where only the pork was left. The Paris synagogue break-in is confirmed; community officials said it appeared to be attempted theft.

- **The twenty-one martyrs.** The February 2015 beheadings on a Libyan beach were of twenty Egyptian Coptic Christians and one man from Ghana, Matthew Ayariga, who is reported to have declared their God his God. A separate Islamic State video in April 2015 showed the killing of Ethiopian Christians in Libya.

- **The “minaret of skulls.”** Towers of skulls are historically associated with Timur (Tamerlane) in the fourteenth century, not with Muhammad. The early Islamic sources do record the beheading of the men of the Jewish Banu Qurayza tribe at Medina.

- **Taqiyya.** The doctrine of permitted dissimulation is primarily a Shia teaching about concealing one’s faith under persecution. How far it licenses deception of non-Muslims is disputed among scholars; the broader record of Islamist movements saying different things in different languages is better documented than the doctrine itself.

- **666 and the shahada.** The suggestion that the Greek letters for 666 in some manuscripts resemble the Arabic of the Islamic confession is a modern speculative reading, not established scholarship. I mention it as a question, not a finding.

- **Border numbers.** Estimates of entries during the Biden administration vary widely depending on what is counted (encounters, releases, evasions). Figures of 14 to 20 million are at the high end of claims and are disputed.

- **Abdul El-Sayed.** He won Michigan’s Democratic Senate primary in August 2026 and faces Mike Rogers in November. I have not seen documentary evidence of a Muslim Brotherhood connection. He does claim allegiance to Islam.

- **Revelation.** The verse on those beheaded for the witness of Jesus is Revelation 20:4.
