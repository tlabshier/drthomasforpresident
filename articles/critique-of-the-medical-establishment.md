**Critique of the Medical Establishment**

By Thomas Lee Abshier, ND

9/26/2023

Below is a [conversation](https://www.onenewspage.com/video/20220919/14939044/Doctor-to-Doctor-with-Dr-Rima-Laibow.htm?utm_source=substack&utm_medium=email) summary between Rima Laibow, MD, and Ana Marie Mihalcea, MD.

– Dr. Milhalcea discussed her journey from practicing conventional medicine to discovering alternative/integrative approaches and the importance of considering health's spiritual/energetic dimensions. Her realization led her to leave the system and open her health center.

– Both doctors agree that the medical system is structured more like a cult indoctrination through abusive practices like sleep deprivation during training. This traps doctors into the system through fear, ego needs, and emotional damage rather than rational thinking.

– They criticized the COVID response and recognized that some clinicians are now complicit in harming patients due to misinformation. However, they can still wake up by re-educating themselves and following their inner sense of right and wrong.

– Clinically, they see "miracles" by removing poisons from patients' bodies and optimizing nutrition/energy medicine to allow the body's innate healing abilities to manifest. Faith in healing comes from within the patient.

– Overall, the discussion emphasized listening to one's inner voice, connecting to a higher purpose in service of others, and recognizing that truth will resonate for those seeking it. Doctors should not be afraid to stand up for ethics regardless of the consequences.

– Both doctors reject the terms "alternative medicine" or "integrative medicine" to describe their approaches. Dr. Laibow prefers the term "advanced medicine" as it focuses on going beyond current limits.

– They see the mind and body as a single unified system, not separate entities connected by psychoneuroimmunology. Intentionality and plasticity allow this unified system to change through inward focus.

– Dr. Milhalcea shared personal experiences like NDEs that deepened her understanding of consciousness, surviving physical death, and how thoughts directly affect the physical body.

– Initial assessments take 3+ hours to understand complex patients failed by previous approaches. Time and care are prioritized over quick assumptions.

– Emotional and spiritual commitment from the doctor aids healing through visualization of what's possible, reflecting optimism the patient can embody.

– Treatments aim to optimize cellular function, remove toxicity, and support regeneration through diverse modalities tailored to each individual. Safety is ensured through graduated protocols.

– Politicians, agencies, and systems that demand harm be done against ethics have lost integrity and sold out humanity. Doctors must find the courage to disobey such demands.

– A growing global doctor's movement is rejecting harmful COVID policies based on emerging collective conscience, offering solidarity and strength in principle.

– Medical training functions like a cult induction by sleep/food deprivation, abuse from superiors, demands for conformity over critical thought and develops an inflated ego/authority complex.

– Doctors may compartmentalize ethical issues or use rationalizations to avoid discomfort, which damages the soul. True healing requires courage to face this discomfort and follow our inner truth.

– Pharmaceutical solutions are viewed as enzyme-poisoning without acknowledgment of side effects. Early teaching emphasized symptom suppression over addressing root causes.

– Feelings of ambition, prestige, or financial security keep doctors trapped in a system causing harm. Inner fulfillment comes from practicing true healing principles regardless of the consequences.

– Natural solutions are consistently suppressed through various means, including co-opting medical systems to demonize alternatives like made-up disease associations.

– 5G/electromagnetic frequencies in homes and environments may damage neurological/immune health beyond content issues through nonconsensual exposures.

– Recent deadly vaccines were pushed using threats to licensing rather than science or ethics. Real-world harms contrast starkly with advertised safety claims.

– Lawsuits could help remedy damages if protections are pierced for willful misconduct. But mass resistance and exiting abusive power structures may have a wider impact.

– Humanity faces an existential threat from those openly stating genocidal plans that conform with suppressing natural health/spirituality in favor of transhumanist control grids. A collective response is urgent.

– By announcing plans openly in media like books, movies, etc. The conspirators believe they absolve themselves of karma if their plans succeed due to public inaction.

– However, if enough people participate, exposing these plans and encouraging specific actions like speaking out, protesting oppressive policies, and refusing to comply can undermine their goals.

– True healing integrates spiritual/energetic healing through addressing negative thought patterns, emotions, or past trauma that manifest physically. Inner transformation enables outer change.

– To withstand intimidation tactics, community support for those exiting oppressive systems or medical dissenters is important. United movements have a more significant protective effect.

– Personal experiences like NDEs, psychic phenomena, or miraculous healings point towards a greater spiritual reality transcending materialism. This offers confidence and perspective.

– The future remains unwritten and dependent on collective human participation. A critical mass of informed people standing on principle for health sovereignty and against totalitarian control grids could shift outcomes positively.

– Overall, having faith in healing from within by listening to inner truth and wisdom, rather than threats or propaganda, was emphasized as the path through this pivotal time. Connection to like-minded others strengthens individual journeys.
