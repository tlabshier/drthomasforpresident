# 261030 - Why the Physics before Politics

# Why a Platform of Physics and Faith


Oct 30, 2026 · @Thomas Lee Abshier, ND


I spend most of my time on physics rather than political articles because my campaign makes a claim that physics can support: the Bible is not one opinion among many, but God’s description of how a real world, built by Him, actually works.


## The question


This week my campaign manager asked a fair question. The position papers are already written, and people respond to them. Why not simply promote those? Do I really need to finish a theory of physics before the campaign goes forward?


I believe I do, and this essay explains why. Physics is not more important than morality. My moral platform rests on a claim about reality, and that claim deserves to be defended where the modern world believes reality is decided.


## A platform without a foundation


My platform is simple to state. Government should be moral, and the standard of morality is the one God revealed in the Bible. Before any law or policy, I ask three questions: Is it righteous? If so, is it loving? And would I want it done to me? A government that ignores these questions is not neutral. It is an organization using force without a moral warrant, which is the definition of a criminal enterprise.


When a candidate says this, most listeners hear a religious enthusiast. They have been taught that science describes the real world and religion describes private feelings. In that picture, the Bible may be inspiring, but it is not knowledge. A man who wants to govern by it is asking the nation to adopt his preferences.


Christians have largely accepted that picture. We defend our faith as a matter of the heart and concede the universe to the physicists. We apologize for Genesis, avoid the question of how God acts in the world, and hope no one asks how a God who cannot be found in nature can still author law. That posture leaves a Bible-based platform without footing in public argument.


## What the physics claims


For about forty years I have been developing a theory I call Conscious Point Physics. It starts with the idea that space is not an empty stage. At the smallest scale, the universe is made of points of awareness that God created and sustains, which I call Conscious Points. Each one perceives its surroundings, computes a response, and moves, over and over, according to a small set of rules. Every particle, force, and field we observe is built from the collective behavior of these points.


If that is right, then two things follow. First, the familiar laws of physics are not independent facts that happen to hold. They are the visible patterns of the rules by which God’s created points behave. Second, wherever you look, at the bottom of a rock, a star, or a human body, you find the mind of God at work. God is not a hypothesis added to physics from outside. He is what physics is describing when it reaches the bottom.


The theory is meant to be judged the way any physical theory is judged: by whether it reproduces what is measured, with few assumptions, and whether it predicts something new that can be checked. So far it reproduces Einstein’s equations of gravity from its own rules, and it predicts faint “echoes” after black holes merge, a prediction that can be tested against gravitational-wave data already recorded. It also offers an account of dark matter that is now being worked through in detail.


## From the bottom of matter to the top of government


If God is present at the bottom of every place, then He is not a distant lawgiver who must be taken on faith alone. He is the one whose mind is running the world, and He would have reason to tell the creatures He made what that world is for and how to live in it. The Bible then reads as what it claims to be: revelation from the author of reality, not a record of human religious feeling.


That revelation addresses life at three levels. It tells the individual how to govern himself. It tells two people how to treat each other, in friendship, in marriage, in business. And it tells a people how to govern themselves together. These are not three separate subjects. They are the same moral order applied at three scales, just as the same physical laws apply to an atom and to a galaxy.


One observation anyone can make, with or without physics, is that when people live by the biblical pattern, their lives tend to work. Marriages hold, children flourish, debts are paid, and communities trust each other. When they abandon it, the costs show up in the same places. The physics does not replace that evidence. It explains why the evidence looks the way it does. A moral law that comes from the one who built reality should fit reality, the way a manufacturer’s instructions fit his machine.


So when I say a government should be judged by whether it is righteous, loving, and something I would accept done to myself, I am not offering a personal taste. I am claiming that this standard is built into the world, and that a nation that departs from it will find reality pushing back.


## Why a scientific result gets a hearing


In 1919, Albert Einstein was respected by physicists but unknown to the public. That November, British astronomers announced that starlight passing the Sun during a solar eclipse had bent by the amount his new theory of gravity predicted. Within days his name was on the front page of *The New York Times*, and he became the most famous scientist in the world. People who could not follow his mathematics wanted to hear what he thought about everything.


The lesson is not that fame is the goal. It is that a confirmed prediction about nature earns a hearing that no amount of argument can buy. People who would never read a position paper on marriage or monetary policy will listen to the man who explained something the experts could not.


Dark matter is such a thing. Astronomers infer that most of the matter in the universe is invisible: galaxies spin too fast and bend light too much to be explained by the matter we can see. For decades, enormous experiments have searched for the particle responsible and found nothing. Everyone has heard of dark matter, and no one knows what it is. A theory that explains it and also unifies the forces of physics that have resisted unification since Einstein would be noticed.


If such a theory also says that God is at the bottom of every place, and that the same order runs from particles to marriage to government, the man presenting it is no longer easy to dismiss as a fanatic. People may still disagree with him. But they may want to know what he says about other topics.


## What the argument rests on


I claim that a physical theory with God at its foundation can stand beside conventional physics and be compared on the same terms: how much it explains, and whether its new predictions hold. I believe Conscious Point Physics will fare well in that comparison, and I am putting it forward to be tested, not to be taken on my word. Some parts are finished, and some are still being worked out, and I will say which is which.


I am not claiming that faith in God depends on my theory. Christians believed long before any physics, and the moral case for my platform stands on its own evidence in human lives. If a prediction of mine fails, my theory needs correcting, not God. What the physics adds is this: it removes the excuse that a scientifically serious person must keep God out of his account of the world. Once that excuse is gone, the biblical standard for government can be argued on its merits.


I hold this position open to a better argument. But I hold it.


## Why the physics comes first now


When I explained this to my campaign manager, she changed her mind. Her question became how to give me more time for physics and less for writing political articles. Since then, I have spent nearly all my working hours on physics.


It is harder work than the essays. Writing a position paper is a craft with room for judgment and word choice. Physics is unforgiving; an equation is either right or wrong, and a great deal rides on getting it right. But I believe it is the work I have been given to do, and no one else is in a position to do it.


The campaign is scheduled to begin on July 4, 2027. Between now and then, my goal is to develop the theory to the point where I can present its central claims to physicists and the public alike. The campaign message will not change: live the Bible, and vote for those who will govern by it. What I hope will change is that unbelievers, upon seeing the evidence and reason of Conscious Point Physics, feel enough confidence to explore the Biblical message enough to understand its claims, follow the logic that extrapolates from the physics, and understand the causal connection between Jesus and how his life connected with the real world.


But that is only part of the campaign audience. The other part is the larger group: those who already call themselves Christians. I believe many Christians hold back from speaking in bold defense of the Gospel because of the concern that their message cannot stand up against the scientists who claim there is no God. I hope this theory will give the Church/Christendom the boldness to stand and declare that God is real, that the Bible’s claims reflect God’s character, and that faith in the Bible is compatible with science, evidence, and reason. If Christians participate, if they vote, we can win and take our country back. We are still a Republic, and our voices still determine who leads our country and establishes our laws.
