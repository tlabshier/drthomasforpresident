Here is a summary of the key points from the discussion:

1. Margo prefers Michele Bachmann over Ron Paul as a presidential candidate. She believes Bachmann is more aligned with Constitutional principles and values based on God's law.

2. Margo argues that the U.S. Constitution is based on Biblical principles and that Founding Fathers saw religion and morality as important supports for the nation. She cites George Washington's farewell address to support this view.

3. John believes that any conservative candidate who supports strict constitutional interpretation is preferable. He thinks Ron Paul upholds the Constitution most consistently, especially regarding issues like the Federal Reserve and reducing the size of government.

4. John questions Margo's claim that Ron Paul voted with Pelosi against extending Bush tax cuts. He says it is inconsistent with Ron Paul's anti-tax, pro-abolishing IRS positions.

5. John argues that government agencies like the military are just as inefficient and prone to corruption as other departments. Margo does not grant the military a "free pass."

6. They generally agree that most of what the federal government does today is unconstitutional. But Margo believes the federal government has a role in encouraging morality, while John thinks government should limit itself to what is strictly constitutional.
