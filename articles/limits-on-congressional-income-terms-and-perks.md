**Congressional Reform to Prevent Corruption**

Internet email chain letter

8/17/2023

* No Pension. Congress collects a salary while in office only.

* Congress participates in Social Security, and each elected official provides for their retirement.

* Congressional pay will rise by the CPI or 3%, whichever is lower.

* Congress participates in the same health care system as the American people.

* Congress must abide by all laws they impose on the American people.

* No insider trading.

Serving in Congress should not be a career. The Founding Fathers intended self-governance by "We the People." Citizen-legislators should serve their terms and return to doing productive work in the private sector.
