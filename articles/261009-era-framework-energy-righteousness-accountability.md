# ERA Framework: Energy, Righteousness, Accountability

Oct 9, 2026 · @Thomas Abshier

## Plain language summary

Thomas introduced a new framing for all presidential content: **ERA — Energy, Righteousness, Accountability**. The discovery came from working through China policy analysis and a 10th Amendment essay with Claude: "loving your neighbor" alone is insufficient as a governing principle — it must include a righteousness standard and accountability structure. ERA is now being incorporated into all new articles as the lens through which policy positions are evaluated.

## The framework

**Energy** — The motivating force. People need wind at their backs. Capitalism provides this through self-interest and family; communism expects saint-level motivation that doesn't exist in practice. The question for governance is: what energy system actually moves people to act for the common good?

**Righteousness** — The moral standard. Love alone is not enough — it must be love directed by a standard of right and wrong. Without righteousness, "loving your neighbor" becomes whatever feels good, which can justify anything. The standard comes from God's character as revealed in scripture, not from human consensus.

**Accountability** — The enforcement mechanism. A righteous standard without accountability is just aspiration. Accountability operates at multiple levels: personal conscience, family, church, community, and — as a last resort — government. The closer accountability stays to the individual, the healthier the society.

## Origin

This framework emerged from Thomas's ongoing work with Claude, specifically:

- A China policy analysis that exposed the inadequacy of "love your neighbor" as a standalone foreign policy principle
- A 10th Amendment essay that clarified the proper hierarchy of accountability (individual → family → community → state → federal)
- The October 5 fellowship discussion on capitalism, socialism, charity, and character — where Thomas articulated that capitalism works because it aligns with human motivation, but unmoored capitalism (without righteousness) drifts toward greed

## Connection to the campaign

ERA provides the structural backbone for the "No King but King Jesus" platform:

- **Energy** answers "why would anyone do this?" — because God made us to thrive in righteous community, and the payoff is real
- **Righteousness** answers "what's the standard?" — God's character, not human consensus
- **Accountability** answers "how do you enforce it?" — primarily through transformed hearts, not government coercion

This maps to Thomas's broader thesis: the presidential campaign is a revival, not a political strategy. ERA is the framework that connects the spiritual transformation ("Christ in every heart") to practical governance (taxes, welfare, foreign policy, healthcare).
